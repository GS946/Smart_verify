## Chapter 1: General Norms & Statutory Ingestion

### Clause ID: SYNTHETIC-DEMO-CH1-CL1.8
- **Category**: Document Integrity & Institutional Identity
- **Academic Year**: 2026-27
- **Version**: 1.0.0
- **Summary**: Statutory filings and verification credentials must belong exclusively to the applicant institution.
- **Rule Text**: Every certificate, NOC, deed, or photographic evidence uploaded in support of an Extension of Approval (EoA) or intake variation must uniquely correspond to the physical campus and legal entity of the applicant institution. In accordance with procedural integrity safeguards, any statutory submission matching document fingerprints or cryptographic hashes previously or concurrently registered to an independent institution shall be flagged for scrutiny officer verification.

### Clause ID: SYNTHETIC-DEMO-CH1-CL2.4
- **Category**: Regulatory Processing Fee (TER)
- **Academic Year**: 2026-27
- **Version**: 1.0.0
- **Summary**: Mandatory Technical Education Regulatory (TER) fee realization.
- **Rule Text**: Applications for Extension of Approval (EoA), introduction of new programmes, or change in intake capacity shall only be cleared for scrutiny upon full realization of the prescribed Technical Education Regulatory (TER) charges in the AICTE central treasury ledger. Where electronic confirmation indicates pending or unverified payment status, the application shall be held in a 'Rectification required' or pending state until payment reconciliation.

## Chapter 2: Statutory Approvals & Safety Certifications

### Clause ID: SYNTHETIC-DEMO-CH2-CL4.1
- **Category**: Fire Safety Standards & NOC
- **Academic Year**: 2026-27
- **Version**: 1.0.0
- **Summary**: Fire Safety Certificate issuance and mandatory validity window.
- **Rule Text**: The institution shall furnish a valid Fire Safety Certificate / NOC issued by the competent Fire Service Authority of the respective State / UT Government. The NOC must remain unexpired through the commencement of the academic session (or have at least 30 days of active validity from the date of submission). Expired certificates or certificates with date anomalies shall result in immediate preliminary non-conformance.

### Clause ID: SYNTHETIC-DEMO-CH2-CL4.3
- **Category**: University Affiliation & Academic Jurisdiction
- **Academic Year**: 2026-27
- **Version**: 1.0.0
- **Summary**: Valid Affiliation Order for the declared academic year.
- **Rule Text**: Technical institutions must possess an unexpired letter or certificate of affiliation granted by the affiliating University for all courses offered. If the affiliation validity has lapsed, or if digital signature inspection reveals post-signing alteration of validity dates or certificate identifiers, the application requires immediate manual inspection referral.

### Clause ID: SYNTHETIC-DEMO-CH2-CL4.5
- **Category**: State Government Concurrence
- **Academic Year**: 2026-27
- **Version**: 1.0.0
- **Summary**: State Government NOC for new institutions or new programmes.
- **Rule Text**: Applications seeking to establish a new technical institution or launch a new programme division must attach the formal No Objection Certificate (NOC) granted by the Department of Technical / Higher Education of the State Government.

## Chapter 3: Programme Intake & Capacity Baselines

### Clause ID: SYNTHETIC-DEMO-CH3-CL5.2
- **Category**: Approved Intake Limits & Record Consistency
- **Academic Year**: 2026-27
- **Version**: 1.0.0
- **Summary**: Consistency of declared intake with central AICTE baseline records.
- **Rule Text**: An institution applying for Extension of Approval (EoA) continuation cannot declare an intake higher than the sanctioned baseline recorded in the AICTE authorized database for the preceding academic year, unless an official application for intake increase has been approved. Any discrepancy between declared intake (e.g. 120 seats) and baseline sanctioned capacity (e.g. 60 seats) without prior approval constitutes a critical cross-record non-conformance.

### Clause ID: SYNTHETIC-DEMO-CH3-CL5.4
- **Category**: Intake Expansion Prerequisites
- **Academic Year**: 2026-27
- **Version**: 1.0.0
- **Summary**: Eligibility criteria for intake expansion.
- **Rule Text**: Additional intake divisions in existing programmes shall only be granted to institutions having valid NBA accreditation for at least one third of eligible courses, adequate faculty-student ratio, and built-up instructional space adhering to AICTE floor area norms.

### Clause ID: SYNTHETIC-DEMO-CH3-CL6.1
- **Category**: Campus Land & Approved Building Plans
- **Academic Year**: 2026-27
- **Version**: 1.0.0
- **Summary**: Legal land possession and building plan approvals.
- **Rule Text**: Institutions must upload certified building plans approved by the local municipal or town planning authority, clearly showing barrier-free access, instructional areas, administrative spaces, and total carpet area conforming to statutory requirements.

## Chapter 4: Quality & Accreditation Safeguards

### Clause ID: SYNTHETIC-DEMO-CH4-CL7.1
- **Category**: National Board of Accreditation (NBA) / NAAC Validity
- **Academic Year**: 2026-27
- **Version**: 1.0.0
- **Summary**: Maintenance of active accreditation status.
- **Rule Text**: Where preferential privileges, enhanced intake, or autonomy allowances are claimed based upon NBA or NAAC accreditation, the uploaded accreditation certificate must be active and valid for the relevant academic session. Lapsed or expired accreditation certificates shall not be accepted for quota or intake enhancements.

## Chapter 5: Infrastructure Evidence & Visual Verification

### Clause ID: SYNTHETIC-DEMO-CH5-CL8.4
- **Category**: Infrastructure Photographic Evidence Triage
- **Academic Year**: 2026-27
- **Version**: 1.0.0
- **Summary**: Clarity, uniqueness, and completeness of campus photo evidence.
- **Rule Text**: Institutions must provide clear, well-lit, non-blurred photographic evidence for core instructional spaces: Computer Centre / IT Lab, Central Library / Reading Room, Specialized Laboratories, Classrooms, and Seminar Halls. Visual analysis serves as an evidence triage tool to assist scrutiny officers. Where photographic files exhibit near-duplicate perceptual hashes (suggesting duplicate photo reuse within the application or across institutions), or fail minimum clarity thresholds, scrutiny officers shall recommend targeted physical or virtual spot verification.
